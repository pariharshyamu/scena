Worlds, not just meshes

createTerrain · heightAt(x, z)

Terrain with ground truth

Seeded low-poly terrain whose exact height function is exported, not just baked into vertices — spawning, scattering and navmesh baking never disagree with the visuals.

scatter · InstancedMesh · lod

Forests in one call

Density-noise clumping, spacing, masks and keep-outs; thousands of props in a few draw calls, with opt-in LOD tiles that collapse distant trees to cones.

createDayCycle · createWater · applyWind

A living world

One timeOfDay drives sun, sky, fog and every lamp and window; lakes get sandy shores; wind sways every scattered plant per-instance.

createVillage · createHouse · assembleKit

Settlements

Seeded hamlets whose windows ignite at dusk, and ASCII maps that become grid-snapped forts — walls, doorways, torches, spawn points.

buildScene(manifest) · extractMarkers

Worlds as JSON

Declare a whole wired world as one plain-JSON manifest, or read spawn_*/route_* markers straight out of Blender exports.

Prop → { object, obstacleRadius }

Props know their gameplay

Every generator returns steering metadata GAMA understands structurally — a road's one curve is its ribbon, its scatter keep-out and its patrol route.

PALETTES · meadow autumn dusk winter

A matched set

One palette themes every prop, the terrain bands, sky, fog, water and roofs together. Retheme a whole world by changing one word.

Deterministic & honest

Same seed, same world — enforced by snapshot hashes. Zero dependencies beyond three; Math.random appears nowhere in the library.

  dayCycle: { dayLength: 120 },
  village: { radius: 9, houses: 5 },
