SCENA — a Gerstner-wave sea
The ocean is a subdivided plane displaced by a sum of Gerstner waves in the vertex shader, so crests peak and troughs flatten like real swell, with analytic normals, whitecap foam and a fresnel sky tint. It reads the terrain's heightAt, so it fades out over the island and foams at the shore — and the little boat rides the swell via the same heightAt on the CPU. Try ?view=boat.
