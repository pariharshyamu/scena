SCENA — flags & banners
Flags, hanging banners and pennants, each a subdivided cloth rippled by a GPU vertex wave — a travelling fold that grows toward the fly, droops under gravity, and carries a seeded phase so no two wave in lockstep. Heraldic devices (cross, saltire, bands, diamond…) are baked as vertex colours; nothing is fetched. They animate themselves from the render loop — no per-frame wiring.
