SCENA — procedural surfaces
The full surface palette — 28 presets from plaster and stone to sand, bark, canvas, bronze, and tiled masonry: brick, cobblestone, ashlar, floor tile and shingle. All the weathering, grain, cavity AO, relief and the mortar grid are generated in the shader from triplanar noise — no textures fetched, every preset carries its own colour, and the world-space grid means abutting boxes align into one continuous wall. Try ?view=grid or ?view=built.
