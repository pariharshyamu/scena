SCENA — procedural surfaces
The full surface palette — 23 presets from plaster and stone to sand, gravel, sandstone, granite, slate, bark, leather, canvas, terracotta and aged bronze/brass. Each block is a single primitive; all the weathering, grain, cavity AO and relief is generated in the shader from triplanar noise, and every preset carries its own colour. No textures are fetched.
