SCENA — procedural surfaces
The full surface palette — 32 presets from plaster and stone to sand, bark, bronze, tiled masonry (brick, cobblestone, ashlar), and Tier-3 snow/moss caps and glowing lava & crystal. All the weathering, grain, cavity AO, relief, the mortar grid, the settled cap and the emissive glow are generated in the shader from triplanar noise — no textures fetched. Try ?view=grid, ?view=built or ?view=wild.
